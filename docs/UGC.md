# 12 UGC videos — scripts, hooks, captions

Vertical, 9:16, 1080p, 10 seconds, one presenter talking straight to camera
with a real shop behind her. Selfie-framed and handheld, the way the format
actually looks.

**Get them:** `bash scripts/download-social.sh` — puts all twelve in
`social/ugc/`, numbered, alongside the process reels and the price ads. The
two presenter reference stills come down with them: keep those, because
feeding one back as an image reference is what makes the next batch the same
face.

## The one thing to know about the framing

She is the shop, not a customer. Every line is Surge Labs telling you
something — a price, an opinion, how the trade works. None of them says "I
ordered from these guys and they were amazing," because that is a testimonial,
and a testimonial from someone who never bought anything is the one version of
this that carries real legal exposure rather than just being a bit fake.

You lose nothing. "Everyone thinks a thousand cards costs four hundred bucks"
is a better hook than any invented review, because it starts an argument in
the viewer's head in four seconds.

## Label them as AI when you post

TikTok and Instagram both require realistic AI-generated content to be
disclosed, and both have a toggle in the upload screen for it. Use it. An
unlabelled synthetic presenter is what gets a business account limited or
pulled, and the label costs you nothing — the AI-content tag has no meaningful
effect on reach for ads-style content, and it is a much smaller problem than
being caught.

## Posting rhythm

Three a week, alternating with the process reels in `docs/REELS.md`. A face
video then a machine video reads like an account run by a person. Twelve face
videos in a row reads like a bot farm.

Best slots: 11am–1pm and 6–9pm Eastern.

## Rules for edits

- **Prices must match `content/packages.ts`.** $99 · $120 · $12 · $399 · $180
  · $45 · +35%. Change one there, change it here.
- **Keep the hook in the top third.** TikTok's own UI covers the bottom right.
- **Cut the first frame if she blinks or resets.** The hook has to land inside
  two seconds or the video is dead.
- **Don't add a fake review, star rating or client count** to any of these.
  The site refuses to invent those and so should the videos.
