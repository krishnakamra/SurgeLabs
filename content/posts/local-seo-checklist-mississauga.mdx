For a plumber in Cooksville or an electrician working out of Meadowvale, the three results in the map pack are worth more than everything below them combined. Getting into that box is a different job from ranking a page, and most of it is unglamorous.

Here is the work, in the order it pays.

## 1. Google Business Profile, done properly

This is the whole game for local, and it is free.

- **Claim and verify it.** Unverified profiles do not rank.
- **Primary category matters more than anything else on the profile.** "Plumber" and "Plumbing supply store" are different businesses to Google. Pick the one that describes what you do, not what you also do.
- **Service area, not just address.** If you work at customer sites, set service areas and consider hiding the street address. A profile with a public address it does not trade from is a liability.
- **Hours, including holidays.** A profile showing "closed" when you are open loses the call to whoever shows open.
- **Photos, regularly.** Vehicles, jobs in progress, the team. Recent photos correlate with activity, and activity is a signal.
- **Services and products, filled in.** Almost nobody does this. It is free text that describes exactly what you sell.
- **Answer questions.** You can ask and answer your own — use it for the things people actually phone to ask.

## 2. NAP consistency, character for character

Your name, address and phone number have to be identical everywhere they appear: the profile, your website footer, your invoices, every directory.

Identical means identical. "Unit 4" and "#4" are different strings. "905-598-3960" and "(905) 598-3960" are different strings. Inconsistent NAP is one of the few things that measurably suppresses local ranking, and it is entirely within your control.

Pick the exact format on your Google Business Profile and copy it everywhere else.

## 3. Reviews, and how to actually get them

Review count and recency both matter. So does whether you reply.

- Ask in person, at the moment the job is finished and the customer is pleased. Nothing beats this.
- Send a direct link. Not "find us on Google" — the short review link from your profile, in a text message.
- Never offer anything in exchange. It violates Google's policy and, in Canada, undisclosed incentivised reviews are the kind of thing the Competition Bureau has taken action on.
- Reply to all of them, including the bad ones, briefly and without arguing. The reply is for the next reader, not the reviewer.
- Never buy them. They are identifiable and the downside is losing the profile.

## 4. A real page for each service, and each city you actually serve

One page listing eight services ranks for none of them. A page about drain cleaning in Mississauga can rank for drain cleaning in Mississauga.

The trap is doing this at scale badly. Ten services across sixteen GTA cities is 160 pages, and if they are the same template with the city name swapped, Google treats the lot as doorway spam — and the penalty lands on the whole domain, not the thin pages.

The rule we work to: a city page has to be written for that city. Real neighbourhoods and industrial areas — Meadowvale, Streetsville, Port Credit, Heartland, the Winston Churchill corridor. A genuine delivery or turnaround line. Questions people in that city actually ask. If you cannot write 250 words that are true of that city and nowhere else, do not make the page.

We publish [the list of areas we cover](/service-areas) and we only build the page when there is something real to say.

## 5. On-page basics that still matter

- **One H1 per page**, carrying the service and the city.
- **Title tag under 60 characters**, with the commercial term first and the brand last.
- **Meta description under 155**, written as an ad, with the phone number if it fits.
- **LocalBusiness structured data** with your real telephone, address, opening hours and service area.
- **Fast pages.** Core Web Vitals: LCP under 2.0s, INP under 200ms, CLS under 0.05. Mobile, on a real connection.
- **Internal links with descriptive anchor text.** "Drain cleaning in Mississauga", never "click here".

## 6. Citations, in moderation

Get listed accurately in the handful that matter: Google, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yelp, YellowPages.ca, and the trade associations or licensing bodies relevant to your work. Add the Better Business Bureau if you are a member.

That is most of the available value. Services selling 200 directory submissions are selling volume in a place where volume stopped counting years ago. Accuracy in ten beats presence in two hundred.

## 7. What to ignore

- **Meta keywords.** Dead for twenty years.
- **Exact-match domains.** `mississauga-plumber-247.ca` is not a strategy.
- **Blog posts written for a word count.** Three genuinely useful pages beat thirty padded ones, and the padded ones now actively hurt.
- **Anyone guaranteeing a number-one ranking.** Nobody controls the ranking. They can control the work.
- **Keyword density.** Write the page for the person phoning you.

## The order to do it in

If you do nothing else this month: verify the profile, fix the NAP everywhere, and ask your last ten happy customers for a review. That is a weekend of work and it outperforms most paid campaigns at this size.

Then build the service pages. Then the city pages, slowly, and only where you have something to say.
